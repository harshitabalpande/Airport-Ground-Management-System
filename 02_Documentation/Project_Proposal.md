# Airport Ground Management System

## 1. Project Title

Airport Ground Management System

---

## 2. Introduction

The Airport Ground Management System is a software application designed to automate and manage airport ground operations efficiently. It helps airport authorities monitor flight schedules, gate allocation, baggage handling, fueling operations, and ground staff activities from a single platform.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Airport ground operations involve multiple tasks such as gate allocation, baggage handling, fueling, aircraft servicing, and flight scheduling. Managing these tasks manually can lead to delays, communication gaps, and operational inefficiencies.

The proposed Airport Ground Management System provides a centralized platform to automate these operations, improve coordination among departments, reduce delays, and increase operational efficiency.

---

## 4. Objectives

- Manage flight schedules.
- Allocate airport gates efficiently.
- Track baggage handling.
- Manage aircraft fueling.
- Assign ground staff.
- Monitor flight delays.
- Generate operational reports.

---

## 5. Scope

The system will manage airport ground operations including flight management, gate allocation, baggage handling, fueling, staff assignment, and report generation. The project focuses on improving operational efficiency and reducing manual work.
